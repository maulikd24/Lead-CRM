# Creative intelligence, compliant ad copy and experiments

Four tabs in the Marketing workspace, behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_CREATIVE_INTEL=1` (default off) and visible only to the **Admin** and **Marketing** roles. Access is decided by the central policy registry (`src/lib/access/policy.ts`, areas `marketing-creative`, `marketing-competitor-ads`, `marketing-experiments` and `marketing-reports`) and nothing else: every page and action calls `requireMarketing(area, action)`, and `access-conformance.test.ts` pins that there is no private role list, no private copy of the marketing gate and no private hashing. Managers, relationship managers and finance get none of it (a deep link to a hidden tab falls back to Overview; actions redirect). The AI switch is an Admin action on the AI registry area (`settings-ai`).

Nothing here publishes an ad, changes an ad account or sends anything. The furthest an ad copy variant can go is **Approved**, and a person takes it from there.

## Insights

Per-ad performance by **funded outcome**, fatigue detection, "what the winners share", and tags.

- Outcomes come ONLY from the canonical `CampaignOutcomeProvider` in `lib/attribution` (see `docs/campaign-outcome-provider.md`): spend, leads, funded customers, revenue and cost per funded customer by campaign and ad. This module has no provider of its own and does no spend, funded or cost arithmetic (`outcome-boundary.test.ts` fails the build if it does). It joins the provider's ad rows to what the ad platforms delivered per ad (impressions, clicks) from the marketing module, by channel, campaign and ad name (`outcomes.ts`). `outcome-contract.test.ts` pins that creative, the director report and Campaign impact report identical funded customers and cost for one fixture. A number the provider does not have is `null` and shows as "Not available"; KYC is not computed per ad.
- Ad-level funded counts need the ad on each lead: its **name** in `ad`, `creative` or `utm_content`, or the platform's `ad_id` for an ad whose delivery is synced. Without it an ad shows the platform's own numbers and says so; nothing is guessed.
- **Fatigue** (`lib/marketing/fatigue.ts`, with the platform delivery data it reads) judges the last 28 days: average daily frequency (only where reach is reported), click-through rate decay and cost-per-lead drift, each against the window before, and only with enough volume. One signal is "Watch", two or more is "Tired", too little data is "Too early". All thresholds are labelled defaults and editable in the Thresholds sheet.
- **What the winners share** (`winners.ts`) takes the third of creatives with the lowest cost per funded customer (each with at least 2 funded customers) and reports tags most of them carry and the rest do not. It needs at least 6 tagged creatives and 15 funded customers, otherwise it says "Not enough data yet".
- **Tags** (format, hook, offer, length, call to action) are entered by a person (confirmed) or suggested as **drafts** by rules or by the draft-only AI path. A draft is labelled wherever it is used, and a suggestion never overwrites a confirmed tag.

## Briefs and copy drafts

One brief (topic, audience, language English or Hinglish, hook style, channels) becomes a **master hook, USP and call to action** plus a variant for each channel format. Validated with zod (`kit.ts`).

- **Channel formats** (`formats.ts`): Google responsive search ads, Meta feed, LinkedIn single image, YouTube video and TikTok in-feed, each with field limits and live character counters. The limits are **defaults written from public platform guidance** and shortened where a platform only recommends a length. Check them against the current specifications before booking; they live in one table.
- **Previews** show roughly how each variant will look. They are approximate and use neutral placeholders.
- **One ad copy check**, `checkAdCopy()` in `src/lib/guardrails` (the planner and the social drafter call the same function next). It EXTENDS the canonical agent guardrails (`lib/agents/guardrails`, through `matchAgentRule` and `findIdentifier`) and never copies them: promises of returns, advice, performance claims and identifiers are the agent rules, plus a short list of ad-only extensions (`AD_COPY_EXTENSIONS`: "invest now", an unqualified "we recommend", "never lose"). Equivalence tests pin that nothing the retired creative rule set or the agent eval corpus refused is allowed, and that no extension duplicates a canonical rule. Ad copy cases are in the agent eval corpus (`ad_*` categories). The rules, for every channel: no guaranteed or assured returns; performance claims only beside the past-performance disclaimer; the risk disclosure present (wording configurable); no testimonials where prohibited (configurable); no urgency or scarcity; no advice; no unverifiable superlatives, hype or gifts for investing; the honesty rule (a number about clients, assets or rankings needs a named source); no unresolved placeholders; the firm's own banned words and regulated-claims list; the shared safety layer used by the messaging agents. Each rule shows pass, fail or not applicable, and a failing rule shows **the exact phrase it matched**. A CTA formula check (one verb, one next step, no pressure) is advisory.
- Every variant carries `complianceStatus` (PASS or FAIL). It is recomputed on the saved text at every gate; a stored result is never trusted. A variant cannot move to Needs review or Approved while any rule fails.
- **Workflow**: Draft, Needs review, Approved. Approval needs the reader's tick, and for copy a person wrote, a **second person** (four eyes; an AI draft has no human author). Four eyes follow the approval setting (default on; an Admin can switch it off for a one-person team in the Reports tab, Settings). Editing withdraws an approval. Every step is an append-only event, the approval is in the audit log, and the same decision is available in the Approvals inbox as the canonical kind `ad-creative` (a read-through adapter over `CreativeVariant`; the legacy path stays authoritative). Steps are emitted through `emit()` as `campaign.creative.drafted`, `.submitted`, `.approved` and `.returned`.
- **Scoring rubric** (`rubric.ts`): six criteria, 100 points, verdict **scale / iterate / rewrite**. A compliance failure overrides any score: the verdict is rewrite and the score is still shown.
- **Brand profile** (`brand.ts`): tone, audience segments, regulated claims, banned words and the mandatory disclaimers as structured context. Versioned: saving creates a new version, and a brief records the version it was checked against. The disclaimer wording and the registration line must be confirmed by the firm's compliance officer.
- **AI path** (`ai.ts`): every call goes through `callModel()`, the AI gateway. Three registered purposes: `creative.brief` (the master hook, USP and call to action), `creative.copy` (the channel variants) and `creative.tags` (tag suggestions), plus the compliance judge, all belonging to the registered agent `creative_drafter` (L3 draft only: no action above L3, nothing is published or placed, approval always). The per-purpose kill switch (the environment flag AND an enabled AgentSetting row), the daily budget, the one redactor, retries and the trace row apply. The model may reply `ABSTAIN: <reason>`; the gateway turns that into an abstain result, and a kill switch or a spent budget is a blocked result: in both cases nothing is drafted and the writer is told why. Only the scrubbed brief and the format list go to the vendor. The disclaimers are added by code, never by the model, and an AI draft is checked by exactly the same rules as one a person wrote.
- **Product Registry seam** (`product-claims.ts`): ad copy that names a product should cite the Product Registry, so a number in an ad cannot drift from the offer document. There is no Product Registry on this base, so instead of a stand-in the checklist shows a "product_claims" check that is **not applicable** and says so in words, and a person confirms product names and figures by hand. When the registry exists, hand its `cite()` to the creative service as a `ProductClaimGuard` (`ServiceDeps.productGuard`); nothing else changes. A guard that throws fails closed.
- **Printable annexure** (`/creative-print/[briefId]`): the approved copy per channel, its checklist, who approved it and when, a fingerprint of the exact text and blank sign-off lines, to attach to the firm's ad-approval process. It is a record; it does not approve or publish. Opening it is audited.

## Competitor ads (manual)

A saved list of competitors, ads added by hand or imported from a CSV (`competitor, copy, format, start_date, link, thumbnail_ref`), cards sorted by days live, hook and offer tags (human or draft suggestions), and **Use as brief reference**.

- Only a manual and CSV provider exists. **No scraper and no vendor is connected.** The `CompetitorAdsProvider` interface lets an official ad library or a vendor be added later, after its terms and its coverage of Indian advertisers are checked.
- A thumbnail is stored as a **pointer** (a web address or a short reference), never the image. A pointer holding embedded image data is refused.
- A reference is inspiration only. The brief receives tags and facts, never the competitor's wording, and a draft that echoes a reference too closely fails the originality check.

## Experiments

An experiment log: hypothesis, deciding metric, smallest lift worth acting on, **sample-size guidance written for low volume** (people per arm, days to finish at the stated volume, the smallest lift detectable in 4 weeks, and advice when the test is not realistic), start and end, decision and learning, linked to campaigns. A small "read a finished test" helper calls a difference only with enough data and says "no difference detected", never "proved equal". Standard two-sided test of two proportions, 5% significance and 80% power.

## Data and privacy

No table here references a customer. Briefs, variants, tags, competitor ads, experiments and settings hold staff-written text and aggregate numbers, so the data-privacy erasure transaction is unchanged; `schema-privacy.test.ts` fails if one ever gains a customer link.
